SoulSync Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect

SoulSync stores journal entries, emotion logs, and practice session data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

2. How We Use Information

All data processing occurs locally on your device. Your journal entries and emotional data are used solely to provide personalized insights and track your progress within the app.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored locally using encrypted device storage. If you delete the app, all data will be permanently removed from your device.

4. Third-Party Services

SoulSync may use third-party services for analytics and subscription management (Apple StoreKit). These services have their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

SoulSync is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app's support channel.
