EQBoostAI Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect

EQBoostAI stores journal entries, emotion logs, and practice session data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

2. How We Use Information

All data processing occurs locally on your device. Your journal entries and emotional data are used solely to provide personalized insights and track your progress within the app.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored locally using encrypted device storage. If you delete the app, all data will be permanently removed from your device.

4. Third-Party Services

EQBoostAI may use third-party services for analytics and subscription management (Apple StoreKit). These services have their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

EQBoostAI is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. AI Services

EQBoostAI uses simulated AI technology to provide emotional intelligence insights and analysis. All data processing for AI features occurs locally on your device. No personal data, journal entries, or emotional information is transmitted to external servers or third-party AI providers.

The AI features in EQBoostAI are designed to assist with emotional awareness and social skill development. They do not provide medical, psychological, or professional therapeutic advice.
