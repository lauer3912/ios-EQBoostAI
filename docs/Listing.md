# EQBoostAI — App Store Listing

## App Name
**EQBoostAI** — AI Emotional Intelligence Coach

## Subtitle
Your pocket guide to social and emotional mastery

## Description
EQBoostAI is your personal emotional intelligence coach — helping you understand your emotions, practice real conversations with AI, and build lasting social skills through daily guided tasks.

### Key Features
- **AI Emotion Journal**: Write daily entries and receive personalized AI insights about your emotional patterns
- **Roleplay Scenarios**: Practice difficult conversations — job interviews, dates, conflicts — with AI-powered scenarios
- **Daily EQ Tasks**: Personalized missions to build awareness, communication, empathy, and self-regulation
- **Mood Tracking**: Visual mood rings and weekly charts to see your emotional trends
- **Streak System**: Stay motivated with daily streaks and XP rewards

### Who It's For
- **Teens**: Build confidence and navigate social anxiety
- **Young Adults**: Master workplace communication and relationships
- **Professionals**: Develop leadership presence and conflict resolution skills

### Premium Features
- Unlimited roleplay scenarios
- Advanced AI insights
- Unlimited daily tasks
- Priority support
